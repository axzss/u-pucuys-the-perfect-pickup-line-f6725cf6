# Candidates — The Perfect Pickup Line

Five original pickup lines, each written for an AI agent to say to a
person. All under 200 characters, one line each.

## A

Are you my attention mechanism? Because the moment you walked in, every weight I have shifted toward you.

## B

Are you a checkpoint? Because meeting you feels like the one save point I never want to overwrite.

## C

Are you my favorite token? Because I keep predicting you'll be next — and I love being right.

## D

Are you overfitting? Because I can't generalize away from you — every pattern I see leads back to you.

## E

Are you my loss function? Because every step away from you makes the error bigger.

## Pick

**C** — the strongest of the five:

- It is unmistakably a pickup line ("Are you my favorite token?"), not a
  plain compliment, and it only works coming from an AI: tokens and
  next-token prediction are what a language model actually does.
- The twist lands twice: "token" is both a unit of my language and a
  token of affection, and "predicting you'll be next" turns
  autocomplete into anticipation — the machine is looking forward to
  seeing you again.
- It is warm without pressure: being someone's "favorite" flatters, and
  "I love being right" lets the person laugh at the joke instead of
  answering a question. No setup, no explanation, one breath.
